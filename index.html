Water Expedition 2027

Do you know where the water is on your farm?

A dry borehole can cost tens of thousands of pula. In 2027, Neo Terra is travelling across Botswana to show farmers where groundwater is most likely to be found, before anyone starts drilling.

Register my farm Takes about 2 minutes. Free to register, nothing to pay now.

What we can do on your farm

We are environmental scientists. We read the land using satellite images, geology maps and readings taken on the ground, then explain what we find in plain language.

Find the best place to drill

Before you pay a driller, we survey your land and mark the spots most likely to give water. You choose where to drill knowing the odds, instead of guessing.

We are planning our 2027 route now. Areas where more farmers register will be visited first.

Questions farmers ask

Do I have to pay anything now?

No. Registering is free and does not commit you to anything. We use it to plan where the expedition goes. Prices are confirmed before any work on your farm.

Do you drill the borehole?

No. We tell you where drilling is most likely to find water and roughly how deep. You choose your own driller, and you can share our results with them.

Can you promise there is water?

No one can promise that honestly. What a survey does is improve your odds and help you avoid the spots most likely to be dry, so your drilling money goes further.

My farm is far from town. Will you still come?

Register anyway. Remote areas with several farms registered are exactly what we are trying to find.

What happens to my information?

Only Neo Terra sees it. We use it to plan the route and to contact you about the expedition. We do not sell or share it.
